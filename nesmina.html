The Practice

Not fast fashion. Not factory-perfect.

Nesmina explores what happens when everyday objects are given more time. A hat, wrap or blanket begins with a material choice, a test swatch and the rhythm of repeated stitches.

The result is intentionally human: subtle variation, tactile surfaces and pieces that feel more personal the longer they are used.
