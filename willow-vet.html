What we do

Everything your pet needs, under one roof

Wellness exams

Nose-to-tail checks with time for your questions. 30 minutes, never rushed.

Vaccinations

Core and lifestyle vaccines, with reminders by text so nothing gets missed.

Dental care

Cleaning and X-rays under safe anaesthesia, with a written estimate first.
