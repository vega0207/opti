Studio

Small team, long view.

We design houses, apartments and workplaces that feel calm on the first day and better after ten years. Most of our clients find us through someone who already lives in one of our buildings.

Light firstEvery plan starts with where the sun comes from at eight in the morning and four in the afternoon.
Fewer materialsThree or four honest materials, used well, age better than ten fashionable ones.
Built to lastWe stay on site through construction and come back after one year to check the details.
Process

How a project runs

  1. 01
    Brief and site visit

    A first meeting on site, free of charge. We listen, measure and talk honestly about budget.

  2. 02
    Concept

    Two or three directions as sketches and simple models, so you can compare before choosing.

  3. 03
    Design and permits

    Drawings, materials and the building permit application, with a cost estimate you can plan around.

  4. 04
    Build and follow-up

    We tender, follow the build every week and return after one year to check everything still works.
