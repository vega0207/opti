Since 1987

A smaller bakery, on purpose.

Marta and Johan Keller opened Golden Crust with one oven, a wooden table and a sourdough starter they carried over from home. Three decades later, their daughter Anna runs the counter every morning — with the very same starter.

We still believe bread should have four ingredients, pastry should be eaten the day it's made, and regulars should be greeted by name. If that sounds like your kind of place, come by. The coffee's hot by 6:45.
